## Playbook

Researched against live sources (not training-data guesswork) on 2026-10-01. See each claim's
source rather than trusting it blind — linked below.

**What actually moves a Google ranking, per current guidance:**
- Backlinks remain one of the strongest signals. The #1 result averages 3.8x more backlinks
  than positions 2–10; 95% of pages have zero backlinks at all.
- Quality beats volume decisively — one link from an authoritative, topically-relevant site
  outranks dozens from low-quality directories. Mass link-building and paid links are
  measurably *weaker*, not just riskier.
- The fastest legitimate win is converting **unlinked brand mentions** into real backlinks.
- A Google Knowledge Panel (photo/bio box in search) needs `Person` schema with `sameAs`
  links to verified profiles *plus* corroborating coverage across ~30+ independent
  authoritative sources. This is a longer-term goal, not a two-week one — don't conflate it
  with ranking #1 for a name search, which is realistic soon.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask Techpoint, Techsudor (×2 articles), TheInnovationSpark to add a link to the new site where they already mention your name | Unlinked-mention conversion — highest-leverage, fastest legitimate win | Not started |
| 2 | Point existing profiles at the site: LinkedIn website field, GitHub README, Udemy instructor bio, Selar storefront, Drips Wave (if it has a website field) | Real, topically-relevant backlinks, foundation-building | Not started |
| 3 | Add X and Medium to the site's `sameAs` schema once those profiles exist and are confirmed | Strengthens the Person-entity signal for a future Knowledge Panel | Blocked on profile confirmation |
| 4 | Publish one genuinely citable piece (e.g. mentorship-cohort outcomes write-up) | Longer-term compounding link bait, not urgent | Not started |

**Explicitly ruled out:** directory spam, bulk guest-post blasts, paid links. The research
confirms these are weaker than a few real links, not just against Google's guidelines.
