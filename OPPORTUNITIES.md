## Playbook

### Highest-fit, apply now (rolling, no deadline pressure)

**Starknet Seed Grant Program** — up to $25,000 in STRK. Rolling applications, no fixed
deadline, ~4 week response time. Eligibility: early-stage teams with an MVP/PoC, actively
involved in the Starknet community or a Starknet hackathon participant. **You're directly
eligible** — AutoRamp is Starknet-native and you authored its full PRD and drove it from spec
to a live product with 490+ transactions. This is the single fastest, lowest-friction match
on this list.
[starknet.io/grants](https://www.starknet.io/blog/announcing-the-seed-grant-program/)

**Stellar Community Fund (SCF) — Build Award** — up to $150,000 in XLM, for developers and
startups building on Stellar, aimed at more advanced projects. Your Stellar/Soroban open-source
record (6,350 Drips Wave points, 30 resolved issues across Wave 4 and Wave 6) is real,
verifiable grounding — worth checking current eligibility specifics since this award targets
shipping a product, not just contributing to others' repos.
[Stellar Community Fund](https://grantedai.com/grants/stellar-community-fund-scf-build-award-stellar-development-foundation-fb8204db)

### Bigger, more competitive — follow-on once the above is moving

**Starknet Growth Grants** — up to $1,000,000 non-dilutive, for teams actively growing the
Starknet ecosystem. A natural next step after the Seed Grant, not a first move.
[starknet.io/grants/growth-grants](https://www.starknet.io/grants/growth-grants/)

### Africa-specific — strongest fit through AgroChain specifically

**Google for Startups Accelerator Africa** — equity-free, up to $100,000 in Google Cloud
credits, mentorship from Google PMs/engineers, explicit priority sectors: fintech, agritech,
edtech, healthtech. AgroChain (you're CEO) fits this directly. Cohort-based — check the next
application window.
[blog.google — Accelerator Africa](https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/company-news/meet-the-15-startups-joining-the-google-for-startups-accelerator-africa-class-10/)

**Google Black Founders Fund for Africa** — equity-free funding (share of a multi-million-
dollar fund), Google Cloud credits, product mentorship, investor introductions, for Black-led
tech startups. Also a strong AgroChain fit.

**Tony Elumelu Foundation Entrepreneurship Programme** — $5,000 non-refundable seed capital
plus 12 weeks of structured business training and mentorship. Smaller, but well-established,
broad eligibility, good credibility signal for AgroChain.

### Hackathons

Directly in your ecosystem: **DoraHacks** has run Starknet-specific events (e.g. a Bitcoin and
Privacy Hackathon by Starknet). **ETHGlobal**'s 2026 schedule includes Cannes (Apr 3–5), New
York (Jun 12–14), Lisbon (Jul 24–26), Tokyo (Sep 25–27) — in-person/hybrid, many with remote
tracks. Given your TheBuidlathon judging experience, entering one yourself (not just judging)
is a credible next step.

Discovery: free aggregators exist (Devpost + ETHGlobal combined feeds, and broader 6-platform
aggregators covering Devpost/MLH/DoraHacks/Devfolio/Unstop/HackerEarth) — worth wiring into
this agent as a scheduled check once verified to work without needing a paid API key.

because it's grounded in something real in `PERSONAL-FACTS.md` — the same discipline as
everything else this agent does.
